Real World · Case Studies

Case Studies

Case study: Mayo Clinic
  • 15 Level 1 Pedestals; consistently full; less than $1,000 per pedestal.
  • 24 Level 2 Stations; ~122 unique visitors per month; ~$8,000 per station; $4 a day.
Case study: Durango, CO
  • Objective: Worked with Durango’s Sustainability Manager to develop policies and city code related to electric vehicle charging infrastructure.
  • Worked alongside the Durango Sustainability Manager and other stakeholders (Colorado Department of Energy, etc.) to advocate for changing statewide policy related to charging infrastructure, specifically a focus on allowing diversified charging solutions (some Level 1 charging in addition to Level 2 charging).
  • Outcome: New statewide regulations were enacted based on our recommendations (March 2025).

Next Steps · Conclusion

Conclusion

  • We need to move beyond the “gas station mindset” and focus on an EV’s strengths.
  • Goal: Ubiquitous charging infrastructure.
  • Level 1 charging can satisfy the needs of the vast majority of drivers.
  • Important:
  • Focus on education.
  • Fund Level 1 charging projects.
  • Charging infrastructure isn’t one-size-fits-all.
